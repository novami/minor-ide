Find and replace

Edit › Find › Find… (⌘F) opens the find bar above the editor; Find and Replace… (⌥⌘F) adds the replace field. The bar shows how many matches there are and which one is selected.

  • Return and ⇧Return, or ⌘G and ⇧⌘G, go to the next and previous match.
  • Options: Aa match case, W whole words, .* regular expression, AB preserve case when replacing.
  • With regular expressions, use $1 for groups and \n, \t in the replacement.
  • Replace All is a single step of Undo.
  • ⌘E uses the selection as the search text, shared with other Mac apps; ⇧⌘E uses it as the replacement.

Each window has its own search.
