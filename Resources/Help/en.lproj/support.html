Feedback and support

Report a bug or suggest an idea

Thank you for testing MinorIDE! Please open an issue on GitHub: describe what you did, what you expected and what happened, and add your macOS version. Choose Help › Report an Issue… to get there quickly.

Contribute code

Developers are welcome: fork the repository, make your change on a branch of your fork and send a pull request. Small, focused pull requests with a test are the easiest to review.
